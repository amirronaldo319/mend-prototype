Homework arguments

The after-school standoff.
Ages 6–8, 9–12, 13–15
Draft, not yet reviewed

What might be contributing

A tired or hungry child has less patience for hard work.
Homework can feel personal when it is difficult, or when they fear getting it wrong.

What to try right now

Offer a snack and a short break first.
Sit nearby and ask: “Which part is the trickiest?”
Cut the task into one small step.

Example wording

“Let us do five minutes and then check how it feels.”
“You do not have to get it right. I want to see you try.”

What to avoid

Taking over and doing it for them.
Comparing them with a sibling or classmate.

Preventing it next time

Agree on a regular time and place.
If it stays a battle, tell the teacher what you are seeing.
Optional next steps
When more support may help
If this keeps happening for weeks, is affecting school, sleep or friendships, or you are worried about your child’s safety or your own reactions, talk to your family doctor or a qualified professional.
